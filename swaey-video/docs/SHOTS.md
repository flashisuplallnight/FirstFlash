# heyswaey film v2 — shot bible

The film mirrors the reference (Sampson Visuals' Base44 film, `ref/hi/f_NNNN.png`, 10 fps,
frame n = t (n-1)/10 s) shot for shot. Same timing (50.5 s), same visual grammar, but every frame
is about Swaey: a voice agent that lives in the Mac's notch. All product imagery is **real-looking
macOS UI built in HTML/CSS** (Sonoma/Sequoia, light appearance unless stated) plus the two real
macOS wallpapers from heyswaey.com. No stock photos.

## Visual grammar (non-negotiable — this is what makes the reference *not generic*)

- **Canvas:** warm cream `#ede8e3`, flat. Dark shots use `#1d1d1d`. "Sunset" backdrop for app
  windows: vertical gradient `#e8e3de 0% → #e7e2dd 35% → #ece2dc 55% → #f3e1d6 70% → #f7e1d2 78% →
  #eadbcf 86% → #c6d1cf 94% → #b6cac9 100%`.
- **Type is small and quiet.** Captions are Inter 400 at ~52 px (never bold, never huge),
  `#111`, letter-spacing −0.01em, centred. One accent word per line in electric blue `#0f7fff`
  (the reference's orange "go?").
- **Glyph confetti:** monospace glyphs `/ // < > * ^ ✓ “ ” ⌥ ~ |` scattered around the words in
  ~#333 with a few in accent colours (electric `#0f7fff`, lavender `#a98bff`, coral `#ff7a45`,
  sky `#7fc4ff`, light grey `#c9c4bf`). They **re-position every 2–3 frames** (stop-motion jitter,
  not smooth motion), pop in/out, never overlap the words.
- **Voice cloud:** tiny mono (Geist Mono 18–20 px) fragments scattered across the frame — things
  people say to Swaey (`play some lofi`, `email sarah`, `remind me at 7`, `dark mode on`, `volume 30`,
  `focus mode`, `“hey swaey”`, `⌥↑`, `whisper.local`, `0 sent`) — with small ●/○ dots and one or two
  fragments tinted (coral / lavender / electric). Mirrors the reference's floating `const express()`.
- **Stop-motion collage:** many app windows/cards at different sizes arranged loosely; the layout
  **jumps** to a new arrangement every 2–4 frames (hold frames, no tweening), with slight overlaps.
- **Iridescent wash:** soft, very blurred blobs of cyan `#9fe6ff`, sky, pink `#f6b3e6`, peach
  `#ffc9a3`, orange `#ff9a4d`, lavender over cream; slow drift. Used as backgrounds and transitions.
- **Thermal map:** a gradient map (luminance → navy/blue → cyan → white → pink → orange) applied to
  an image/UI; the reference's "thermal" look.
- **Outlined pills:** 2 px black outline, fully rounded, Inter 400 ~50 px, text types inside and the
  pill grows with it.
- **Node grid:** thin white lines on cream with white dots at intersections (bento backdrop).
- **Dot matrix:** a grid of tiny outlined icons on `#1d1d1d` that change state.
- **Cursor:** the macOS arrow, black with white outline, small (≈ 28 px tall at 1080p), clicks with
  a quick press.
- **Camera:** gentle push-ins/drifts, hard cuts on the beat, short motion-blurred whips. Things are
  often shown *small* with lots of negative space, then cut tighter.

## Brand

Swaey's face mark (white `^ ^` caret eyes + `✓` smile, glow) on a navy rounded square = app icon.
Wordmark `heyswaey` lowercase, Inter 600, letter-spacing −0.05em, ~96 px for the logo lockup.
The island = black notch pill with a blue under-glow, face top-left, waveform, white words.

## Sound

128–136 BPM original synth track, quiet glyph ticks for S01–S02, the first impact on the orb→logo
(4.4 s), energetic through S14, low/airy S15–S19, rebuild S20–S22, near-silence S23–S25, second lift
S26–S31, soft outro. Every click/type/whoosh cue comes from `E.cue()` in the shot code.
